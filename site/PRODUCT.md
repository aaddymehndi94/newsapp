## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated (by the lead engineer's brief): static HTML generated by plain TypeScript run directly on
Node 24 (type stripping, no bundler, no framework), hand-written CSS and one small vanilla JS file.
Served as Cloudflare Workers static assets at measurededition.com (and www; the workers.dev URL still works). Open to search engines since 2026-11-24: every page names its measurededition.com URL as canonical, and `sitemap.xml` lists the editions and stories. The 404 page and fixture builds stay `noindex`.

## Users

One primary reader for now: the owner, living in Bengaluru, who today reads 6–7 news sites a day
(Reuters, BBC, The Hindu, Indian Express …) to assemble an unbiased picture. Reads mostly on a
phone, also on a desktop. Reads once a day, matching the 05:00 IST edition — early
morning and evening.

Their job: get through the day's headlines quickly, see a picture and a little extra for each,
open the stories that matter, read a clean, well-sourced article, and return to exactly where they
were on the front page to keep going.

## Product Purpose

Measured Edition replaces the 6–7-site routine with one calm, ad-free edition published every morning. A
pipeline reads reporting from outlets across the political spectrum, an AI editor clusters and
picks a balanced slate (~70 stories across India, World, seven cities, Business, Science & Tech,
Sports, Health & Environment and more), and an AI writer produces neutral, wire-style articles with key
facts, background, why it matters and — only on contested stories — how it is being framed.

Success: the owner stops opening the other sites, because Measured Edition is faster to skim, pleasanter to
read, and visibly balanced.

## Positioning

A finite, fixed edition (not a feed), balanced by topic and by viewpoint, with every story's
sources and their leanings shown on the page. No personalisation by design: everyone reads the same
edition, which is the point.

## Operating Context

- One edition a day at 05:00 IST (owner's choice, 2026-11-24, to keep costs down). Times are shown in IST.
- Sections: India, World, your city (Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Kochi, Kolkata or Mumbai; three stories each, one section with a city picker), Weather (city forecasts, air quality, satellite view), Business, Markets (Sensex, Nifty 50, S&P 500, Nasdaq, USD/INR, gold, silver, bitcoin with month charts), AI & Software (news plus new GitHub repos, trending Hugging Face models, Show HN launches), Science & Tech, Health & Environment, Entertainment (Page 3: films, music, fashion, celebrity news, Apple Music charts, new and upcoming films), Sports.
- Static pages; browser back restores scroll position natively.
- Pictures are hotlinked from the originating outlet (og:image) or Wikimedia Commons, always
  credited and linked; when none is usable a typographic card stands in.
- Content is machine-written from outlet reporting and must be treated as untrusted input
  (escaped; only `*italic*` and http(s) links allowed in body text).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Front page: lead story, 2–4 secondary leads, then sections in edition order; a section jump bar
  (anchors only, not filters); switch between the day's morning/evening editions; archive.
- Article: picture with credit, headline, dek, dateline/IST time/reading time, key facts, body,
  background, why it matters, framing box (contested stories only), click-to-load video, sources
  with leaning labels, next-in-section and other top stories.
- Read stories are dimmed on the front page (per-browser localStorage, nothing sent anywhere).
- Your city: the reader picks a city once in the city section and it's remembered in the browser
  (nothing sent anywhere). The section and its tab then show only that city, and Weather lists it
  first. With no pick, or no JavaScript, every city shows. City stories never lead the edition.
- Light and dark themes; follows the system by default, with one toggle.
- Works without JavaScript.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Measured Edition**, at measurededition.com (owner's choice, 2026-11-24; "Meridian" was the working name). Written as two words; never "The Measured Edition".
- The owner asked for a **classy**, calm, trustworthy, premium-newspaper feel, with dark and light
  themes. Not a social feed, not a startup landing page.
- **Every headline with a relevant picture** — pictures are central.
- **Look (owner's choice, 2026-11-24): the standard news app, played straight**, at the polish of the
  BBC News app and the NYT / The Hindu apps: serif headlines in a clean app layout, real tabs, true
  dark mode (neutral near-black, no grey-blue). Not a themed or metaphor-driven look.
- Front page shows **a thumbnail for every headline** and a **3–4 line summary**.
- Read stories get a **tick**; nothing is greyed out or dimmed.

## Product Principles

1. **An edition, not a feed.** Finite, ordered by editorial judgement, the same for every reader.
2. **Balance is shown, not claimed.** Sources and leanings are visible on every story; framing is
   described calmly, never toggled.
3. **Pictures carry the skim.** Every headline gets an image or a deliberate typographic stand-in.
4. **Never lose the reader's place.** Opening a story and coming back is effortless.
5. **Nothing between the reader and the news.** No ads, no tracking, no popups, minimal JS.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in both themes, semantic landmarks, visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion`
respected, works at 320px width and without JavaScript.

## Anti-goals

Personalisation, "for you" feeds, follow-topics, per-story left/right toggles, option sprawl,
comments, accounts, push notifications, ads, cookie walls, AI-generated photos of real events.
